Diagnosis

Where the budget is leaking

Four things I noticed, in plain terms. For each: what I saw, why it costs you, and the fix.

Leak 01

Ads live, catalog sold out

NoticedAds are driving clicks while almost the entire catalog shows Sold Out.
Costs youPaid clicks land on products no one can buy, and Google can drop most of the feed on availability, so Shopping and PMax have little to show.
The fixSync live stock to the feed and stop serving unavailable products automatically.
Leak 02

Feed quality and policy risk

NoticedThe feed carries non product items and lookalike fragrance names.
Costs youThese trigger Merchant Center disapprovals and weak matches, so your best products stay buried.
The fixClean the feed, rewrite titles around what buyers actually search, remove policy risk items.
Leak 03

ROAS you cannot see

NoticedTracking is in place, but purchase value is not clearly confirmed end to end.
Costs youIf revenue value is not passed correctly, ROAS cannot be measured or optimized, the one number you care about.
The fixVerify GA4 and Google Ads conversions with real revenue, so bidding optimizes to ROAS.
Leak 04

Two buyers, one funnel

NoticedRetail hobbyists and wholesale buyers are served by the same campaigns and pages.
Costs youTwo very different buyers compete for the same budget, so both convert below potential.
The fixSplit them at the keyword, campaign, and landing page level. See the next tab.
Segmentation

One store, two very different buyers

Your store serves two people whose intent is almost opposite, so I would run them as two separate engines. Switch between them below to see how each one is built.

Who they are

DIY hobbyists and gift makers buying kits, molds, and small scents. Higher volume, lower order value.

Search intent
candle making kitsoy wax for beginnershow to make candlessmall batch fragrance oil
Engine

Shopping and Search on retail collections, plus a discovery PMax fed with clean assets.

Landing page

Category and kit pages, fast and mobile first, priced clearly.

Negatives

Finished candles, branded candle names, jobs, information only searches.

Conversion

Online purchase, tracked with revenue value for true ROAS.

Who they are

Small candle and soap businesses buying in bulk to resell. Lower volume, much higher order value and repeat rate.

Search intent
bulk soy waxwholesale fragrance oilfragrance oil by the casecandle supplies distributor
Engine

High intent Search on commercial terms, plus Customer Match built from your existing buyer list.

Landing page

A dedicated wholesale page. You do not have one today, I would build it.

Negatives

Single, sample, beginner, and retail gift terms that waste bulk budget.

Conversion

Bulk order or wholesale enquiry, tracked as a high value action.

Key gap: there is no wholesale landing path on the store today, even though bulk buyers are half the goal. Building that is one of the fastest wins here.
